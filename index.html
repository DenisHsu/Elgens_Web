Rugged Naval Systems | DNV Type Approved | IEC 60945 Certified

ELGENS provides rugged hardware and software solutions for naval mission systems, including displays, Panel PCs, and edge computing platforms for mission-critical shipboard applications. Trusted by 77 customers across 28 countries, ELGENS platforms are DNV Type Approved, IEC 60945 certified, and compliant with MIL-STD-461G, MIL-STD-704F, MIL-STD-810H, MIL-STD-1275E, and MIL-STD-3009. LPC-P Series Naval Panel PCs

Built for shipboard command, control, and monitoring environments, the ELGENS LPC-P Series delivers reliable computing and visualization for naval mission systems. Featuring IEC 60945 certification, fanless operation, IP65 front protection, anti-glare glass, dual 2.5GbE LAN, multiple I/O interfaces, and 12 to 28V DC input, the LPC-P Series supports seamless integration into bridge systems, combat information centers, and onboard control stations. Powered by Intel® 14th / 13th / 12th Gen 35W LGA1700 processor options and operating from -30°C to 70°C, it is proven in naval applications across Asia-Pacific, Europe, and beyond.

NVIS Display Integration Kit for Naval Night Operations

The ELGENS NVIS LCD Development Kit provides a flexible platform for integrating NVIS-capable displays into naval, defense, and tactical systems. Featuring a dual-mode backlight design, it delivers high daylight visibility and MIL-STD-3009 NVIS Green B compliance for night operations. With an LCD controller board, LED driver, and configurable power and control options, the kit streamlines evaluation, prototyping, and integration for naval bridge systems, combat information centers, and mission-critical display applications.

TDM-P Series Military Naval Touch Monitors

The ELGENS TDM-P Series is designed for naval and maritime defense applications, including bridge systems, combat information centers, deck control stations, and onboard monitoring platforms. Featuring P-Cap touch technology, -40°C to 80°C wide-temperature operation, IP65/IP66 front protection, VGA / DP / HDMI inputs, panel mounting kits, and DC 12V power input, it delivers reliable mission-critical visualization for operations at sea.

Shelby Series Quantum-Resistant Gateway for Defense Mission Networks

The ELGENS Shelby G5135 Series enables defense organizations to strengthen mission networks with Post-Quantum Cryptography-ready protection—without replacing existing infrastructure or redesigning system architecture. Built on a high-performance 1U platform, it provides a practical path toward quantum-resistant cybersecurity for shipboard communications, C4ISR networks, command systems, tactical edge networks, and critical defense infrastructure.

Through PQC-enabled Hybrid TLS and Reverse Proxy architecture, the G5135 simplifies secure deployment while maintaining compatibility with existing network environments. Purpose-built for mission continuity, it integrates 10GbE dual-port optical hardware bypass, High Availability support, and 1+1 redundant power to enhance service resilience in mission-critical operations.
