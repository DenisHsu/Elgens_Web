TDM-B430WD-9

The TDM-B430WD-9 is a 4K Ultra HD marine-grade display featuring a stunning 3840 x 2160 pixel, 43" screen designed to reduce errors and improve operational efficiency. Exceptional image quality, clarity, and sharpness ensure that every detail on the screen is rendered with unprecedented precision — approximately four times that of Full HD. The display is compliant with DNVGL-CG-0339, IEC60945, and IACS-E10 standards for marine and offshore environments.

Product Highlights - Advantages and Benefits
  • 4K Ultra HD Resolution: 43" LCD with 3840 x 2160 resolution for exceptional image quality, clarity, and sharpness.
  • Marine-Grade Certification: Compliant with DNVGL-CG-0339, IEC60945, and IACS-E10 standards.
  • Flexible Video Inputs: VGA / DP x2 / HDMI x2 input for multi-source integration.
  • Wide Range Power Input: DC 9~36V wide range power input.
  • Convenient Front-Panel Control: Optimized 5-key OSD function buttons on the front side for simple operation and quick access to central controls.
  • Flexible Installation: VESA mount / Flush mount options.
  • Reliable Operation: 0~50°C operating temperature range.
